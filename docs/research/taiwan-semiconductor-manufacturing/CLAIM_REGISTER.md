## Epistemic ceiling — why nothing is `established`
`established` requires a **contemporaneous documentary** supporting source, or **two mutually
independent** documentary/academic/reputable_press sources agreeing. Here:
- the richest accounts are **subject-authored** (TSMC/UMC filings, ITRI's own history, Chang's
  oral history) — none contemporaneous;
- the **independent** academic sources **conflict on key dates** (G1).
So every factual claim carries `well_supported`; interpretive claims are `well_supported` on an
expert academic source. This mirrors the Netherlands Research Pack 1 precedent.
