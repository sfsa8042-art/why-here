## Provenance rulings
- **Subject-authored / retrospective**: Chang (participant), ITRI, TSMC and UMC (the firms'
  own accounts/filings). Provenance information, not a verdict of unreliability — but the
  direct-`established` route (which needs a *contemporaneous* documentary source) is not met
  by any of them, so claims resting on them stay `well_supported`.
- **Independent scholarly**: Saxenian and the NRC panel (both `academic`). Lai/Chang/Shyu is
  independent but its **venue could not be verified**, so it is classified `other`, not
  `academic`, and is used only as non-load-bearing corroboration (G10, SUPPORT_AUDIT.md).
- The apparent date disagreements with the subject-authored accounts are reconciled **by event
  type** in DATE_RECONCILIATION.md (mostly different events; residual uncertainty retained).
- **Not registered** (cited *inside* inspected sources, not directly inspected): Wade (1990),
  Callon (1995), Industrial Economics Research Center (1987). They never count as independent
  corroboration (G9).
- **Not used**: company marketing pages, blogs, SEO summaries, Wikipedia, AI-generated text.
  Web figures for TSMC's cap table were rejected for lack of an inspected authoritative source (G2).

## Verification basis for locators
- PDFs: page numbers are the printed page (Chang: "Page N of 18"; Saxenian/NRC/UMC/TSMC/Lai
  by page/section). Verbatim quotes were read from the extracted text this session.
- HTML: section headings + verbatim quoted passages read from the retrieved page.
