Support pattern legend: **(a)** one source · **(b)** multiple insider (subject-authored) sources —
not independent · **(b\*)** insider evidence chain plus an independently-*published* source whose
underlying evidence chain is unclear · **(c)** one independent + one subject-authored · **(d)**
two genuinely independent sources.

**Publisher independence ≠ evidence-chain independence.** A source can be independent of the
subject as a *publisher* (`subjectRelationship: independent`) yet not constitute an independent
line of evidence if its account may itself derive from the same institutional histories.
