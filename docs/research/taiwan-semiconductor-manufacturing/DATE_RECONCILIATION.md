# Taiwan × Semiconductor Foundry Manufacturing — Date Reconciliation (Stage 11A hardening)

The apparent 1975/1976, 1979/1980 and 1979/1980 "conflicts" are reconciled **by event
type**. In each case the sources appear to date *different* events (a decision/negotiation, a
signature, an incorporation, a groundbreaking, an official opening), so most are not direct
contradictions. Where a genuine disagreement remains it is **retained** as a limitation and the
claim stays below `established`. Nothing is silently chosen.
