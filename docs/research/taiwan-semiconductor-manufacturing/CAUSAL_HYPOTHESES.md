# Taiwan × Advanced Semiconductor Foundry Manufacturing — Causal Hypotheses (Stage 11A)

**None of these is a claim.** They are hypotheses to be tested in later research. This pack
authors **no causal claims** — a documented historical sequence is not, by itself, a cause.
Each hypothesis lists what evidence would be needed before it could be authored as a properly
limited causal claim (which would also require stated limitation claims and an independent
supporting pair including an academic source).

## H1 — Public technology acquisition seeded private capability
*Hypothesis:* the ITRI/ERSO route (RCA transfer → demo fab → UMC/TSMC spin-offs) transferred
capability that private firms could not have acquired alone at the time.
*Supports (documented sequence only):* `tw-f-rca-contract-1976`, `tw-f-demo-fab-1977`,
`tw-f-umc-1980`, `tw-f-tsmc-erso-transfer`, `tw-i-state-acquire-transfer-role`.
*Needed:* evidence on the counterfactual private path; independent analysis of dependence.

## H2 — The foundry model fit Taiwan's factor endowment
*Hypothesis:* pure-play foundry succeeded *because* it matched Taiwan's manufacturing strength
while sidestepping weakness in design/marketing/IP.
*Supports:* `tw-f-chang-foundry-rationale` (a participant's stated reasoning — not proof).
*Needed:* independent (non-participant) analysis; comparison with IDM/design paths that failed
or were not attempted.

## H3 — Anchor demand from fabless firms made the model viable
*Hypothesis:* the rise of fabless design houses provided the stable demand the foundry needed.
*Supports:* `tw-f-tsmc-dedicated-foundry`; Chang p.13–14 (retrospective).
*Needed:* contemporaneous customer/order evidence (currently a gap, G5).

## H4 — Clustering / ecosystem self-reinforcement
*Hypothesis:* co-location in Hsinchu (labour pools, suppliers, universities, returnees) produced
increasing returns.
*Supports (context):* `tw-f-hsinchu-park-1980`, `tw-f-umc-first-transfer`,
`tw-i-returnee-reversal` (1990s).
*Needed:* mechanism-level evidence (supplier concentration, labour mobility) tied to outcomes.

## H5 — Broad-based industrial policy vs national-champion policy
*Hypothesis:* Taiwan's pro-entry/SME policy produced a more competitive, resilient sector than a
national-champion strategy would have.
*Supports:* `tw-i-broad-industrial-policy` (interpretive).
*Needed:* comparative evidence (e.g. Korea); explicit counterfactual method (RQ6, gap G7).

## Discipline
Before any of H1–H5 becomes a causal claim it must: (1) rest on an independent supporting pair
incl. an academic source; (2) carry ≥1 sourced limitation claim; (3) survive the "sequence ≠
cause" test. Counterfactuals additionally require a stated analytical method and a speculative marker.
