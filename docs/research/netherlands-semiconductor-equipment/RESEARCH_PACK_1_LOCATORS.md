## 4. Claims qualified or contradicted

- NL-F-006 (≈120M guilders needed): qualified. The dissertation gives 100M guilders as the estimate at Philips's October 1982 partner search (L5); S1's ~120M is attached to 1983. Not a contradiction — different moments — but the claim must be re-drafted as time-indexed ("estimated at 100M in late 1982 (van Duijn); ~120M by 1983 (ASML)") or split in two.
- NL-F-013 sequencing: qualified as above — Del Prado–Philips–Ministry contact predates his knowledge of the stepper.
- S1's Gjalt Smit sequencing: qualified. S1 places Smit's arrival among the pre-founding negotiation steps; the dissertation dates the hire to Spring 1984, i.e., around/after founding (L12). Any timeline claim about Smit should follow the archival-based dating.
- NL-C-004: qualified. The transferred technology was documented as defective within one month (L8/L9), and the new venture immediately re-planned around a successor machine (L13/L14). Inherited capability was real but its product embodiment was not viable — the claim's statement should distinguish capabilities (engineering practice, people, research access) from the PAS 2000 itself.
- Aggregator "2.1M guilders each" capital figure: now contradicted by the contribution agreement (~7M each, L11). Our exclusion of it is vindicated; it must never enter the corpus.
- X1 (independence dating): the dissertation frames 1988 as ASM's exit only, supporting the resolution that 1988 ≠ independence/IPO.

## 6. Identified limitation claims

- NL-L-004 (new, critical) — The dissertation's research was financed by Arthur del Prado and Stichting ADP, with ASM International co-funding publication, and rests substantially on Del Prado's personal archive. (L19 + Introduction's "Historical resources.") This does not disqualify it — the archival documents cited are contemporaneous and often against ASM's interest — but it must attach to every claim resting solely on S4, and it deepens NL-L-001.
- NL-L-005 (new) — For ASML-internal history, van Duijn explicitly defers to Raaijmakers (L7, L3, L13, L14). S3 and S4 are therefore content-dependent for ASML-specific matters, not merely publisher-linked. Consequence for the causal-claim rule: an S3+S4 pair does not constitute an independent pair for ASML-internal claims. Independent second legs must come from the archival documents themselves (which the corpus can cite as distinct primary sources via the dissertation's exact references), from Philips-side records, or from genuinely third-party scholarship.
- NL-L-002 — reinforced: the dissertation shows corporate, entrepreneurial and state strands operating simultaneously (L1, L2, L5, L6) without isolating their weights.
