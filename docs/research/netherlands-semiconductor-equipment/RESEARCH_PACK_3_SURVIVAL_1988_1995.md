# Research Pack 3 — Dossier
## Netherlands × Semiconductor Equipment · ASML's survival and transition, 1988–1995

**Framing finding, stated first because it governs everything below.** The primary uploaded source (van Duijn, S4a) is an **ASM International** history. After ASM's July 1988 divestment it follows ASM, not ASML. Its post-1988 ASML material is therefore sparse, retrospective-analytical, and concentrated in two later chapters — nothing like the archival density of the founding period. Pack 3's evidence base for 1988–1995 is consequently **thin and lopsided**: one directly verified official EU record, a handful of dissertation passages, and otherwise leads. I have not compensated by inflating what exists. **No causal claim in this pack meets the two-independent-lines-plus-academic standard, so none is proposed.**

## 2. Verified chronology (only directly inspected items)

- **31 Jul 1988** — ASM withdraws; Philips takes the 50% for 8.6M guilders *(S4a, Pack 1 L17; production claims 9–10)*.
- **1 Nov 1988** — **DEEP-UV (ESPRIT II, grant 2048) begins**, ASM Lithography as coordinator, Veldhoven address (Meierijweg 15) *(S12, verified)*.
- **1988–1991** — DEEP-UV runs; deliverables reported: deep-UV stepper prototype with 5× all-quartz optics, through-the-lens alignment referencing the reticle, demonstrated overlay <125 nm and later 60 nm, resolution 0.35 µm (single-layer resist) / 0.25 µm (top-surface imaging), 248 nm excimer illumination *(S12)*.
- **31 Oct 1991** — DEEP-UV ends *(S12)*.
- **1993** — AMTC dissolved, A600 mothballed; ASM-side write-offs *(S4a pr. 504)* — ASM context, not ASML.
- **1995** — **ASML IPO**: company received ~90M guilders; valuation ~990M guilders *(S4a pr. 504, fn 155 → ASML Annual Report 1995 + Raaijmakers p. 636)*.
- **1995 onward** — Philips realized almost 200M guilders diluting its stake; Dutch government investments described as reimbursed *(S4a pr. 504, fn 156 → Raaijmakers p. 636)*.

Everything else in the requested chronology (positions immediately post-1988, PAS 5500 development, customer relationships, 1992 loan, Philips board deliberations, conditions of Philips's exit) is **not verifiable from inspected material**.

## 11. Evidence complicating each hypothesis

- **H1:** ASML's post-1988 development occurred inside a multi-party European project in which Philips was one participant among seven — complicating, not disproving, a Philips-centric account.
- **H2:** CORDIS publishes no funding figures for DEEP-UV, and the verified Dutch instruments (TOK/INSTIR, L24) attach to the *pre-1988* period; policy support is documented as present but not sized.
- **H3:** The DEEP-UV prototype's specifications (0.25–0.35 µm, 248 nm) sit at a different technical target from the PAS 2500's 0.7 µm, so a simple linear model-to-model survival narrative is unsupported.
- **H4:** Participation lists show relationships but not dependency; nothing establishes ASML could not have sourced optics or process knowledge otherwise.
- **H5:** Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence — the AMD lead predates 1988 and comes through Raaijmakers.
- **H6:** Untested; no governance evidence inspected.
- **H7:** Untested in this pack.

Edition/date inconsistency inside S4a — must be recorded, and any future Raaijmakers citation must specify edition.
